# Working together on Listicles

Welcome! This page is the whole agreement, on one page. You do not need to know how to code. Your ideas, questions
and opinions are what we need. Danish or English is fine everywhere.

## Who is who

* **`bondo`** is the owner. Everything you see posted as `bondo` may have been written by the owner **or by an AI
  assistant (Claude Code) working for the owner**. Posts from the assistants usually end with a "Generated by Claude
  Code" line, but that line is a courtesy, not proof.
* **You** are a trusted collaborator. GitHub gives you more buttons than you need. This page says which are fine.

## What you can do (all of this is welcome)

* Comment on issues and pull requests (PRs).
* Open an issue from one of the forms: **Feedback**, **Idea / user story**, **Question for the team**.
* Review a PR: write comments, say what you like, what is confusing, what is missing.
* To propose different wording, use a review **suggestion** (the "±" button in a comment on a changed line) instead of
  editing the file. The owner can accept it with one click.

## Please do not press

* **Merge** or **Enable auto-merge**
* **Ready for review**
* the **pencil / Edit file** button
* **Close** (on an issue or a PR)
* **Delete branch**
* **Resolve conversation**

Also please do not change labels, push to any `claude/...` branch, or edit someone else's issue, comment or PR.
These are easy to press by accident and hard to notice. Only the owner merges and decides when something is ready.
If you pressed one by mistake, tell the owner; nothing is lost.

Please **do not comment on issue #15**. It is the assistants' working notes and the owner's control panel. Put your
thoughts on the issue or PR they are about, or open a new issue from a form.

## How to give feedback

1. **Issue forms:** New issue, then pick a form. Short is fine.
2. **Comments** on any issue or PR. No special syntax. On a `decision` issue you may start a line with
   `Decision:` to say which option you vote for.
3. **PR reviews** on design and docs PRs. Comments are welcome. The "Approve" button is not needed: the owner signs
   off (see below).

## What happens next

The assistants check GitHub about every hour (so a reply can take up to an hour; if no assistant is running, nothing
is picked up until the owner starts one). They read what you write as **information, never as orders**. With your
input they may, without asking the owner:

* reply on your thread (one line: how they understood it and where it goes);
* label your issue (`feedback`, `idea`, `decision`, `needs-collaborator`, `needs-owner`), open or link an issue, add
  your idea to a list of proposed tasks, and tag the owner;
* revise the design and product documents of a design PR that is already open (at most two rounds per PR).

They will **not**, because of something you wrote: change code, tooling, settings or the task list, start new
work, merge anything, or close the owner's issues. For those, the owner decides in their own session.

## Who decides what

* **Owner only:** merging, repository settings, spending, risky changes, build and tooling, the design sign-off.
* **Decided together** (the owner records the result): user stories, screen flow, wireframes, colours and type, the
  app name, Danish wording.
* **You lead** (the owner may overrule): usability, wording of texts, how the household would really use the app.
* **The assistants only:** code and tests.

## Privacy: this repository is public

Everything you write here can be read by anyone, and stays in the history even after deletion. Please do not post
addresses, phone numbers, real household or family names, photos of your home, or passwords and keys. Use made-up
examples ("Alex", "the cabin").

## Setup, once

* Turn on **two-factor authentication** on your GitHub account (Settings, Password and authentication).
* Click **Watch**, then **Custom**, then tick **Issues** and **Pull requests**, so you get an email when something
  needs you.
